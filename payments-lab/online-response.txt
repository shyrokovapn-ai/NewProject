Uklon uses streaming for seconds-level ride matching, driver locations, surge pricing, and alerts, while batch supports dashboards and model training with older data. A 10-minute stream delay makes decisions stale; a one-day batch delay mostly makes reports and training data stale. MQTT/IoT Core suits persistent, frequent GPS pings better than invoking Lambda for each ping. A small startup can begin with one service, a relational database, object storage, and scheduled SQL, accepting less independent scaling and real-time state.

On the October payments dataset, Athena found 3,030,197 webhook events, 1,000,000 unique invoices, and 30,197 exact duplicate rows. 60,824 events (2.01%) arrived more than 60 seconds late; none arrived more than an hour late. Deduplicating exact event rows before row_number preserves real status changes while preventing redeliveries from inflating counts. The Glue crawler inferred integer amount/currency values, string dates and failureReason, and recognized dt as a string partition.

For turnover, failure rate, and peak hour, the three JSON queries scanned 1.261 GB each; the Parquet versions scanned 4.992 MB, 0.570 MB, and 3.776 MB. Total scan volume fell 405×, and all three Parquet query runtimes were lower. The top turnover group was merchant m-009 in UAH at ₴310,259,259.21; QR on Android had the highest failure rate at 6.71%; the peak was 80,338 payments at 18:00 UTC.

In the local Redpanda run, the full 20-payments/s baseline with 120 seconds of grace rejected 1,651 late events. With the same 20-payments/s replay, zero grace rejected 2,025 events (374 more) and admitted 135 fewer terminal outcomes. The producer selected exactly 10,008 of 33,361 payments (30.00%) for failure injection; the terminal-outcome failure share was 34.07% and triggered 3,407 alerts. A one-minute consumer pause did not lose records: after restart the group caught up to all 101,084 records. Two group members split the three partitions.
